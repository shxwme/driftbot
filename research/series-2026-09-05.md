Date adapter requirements:

- The tables include event-operation ranges starting Friday for most double rounds; the [official navigation](https://d1gp.co.jp/) generally advertises Saturday–Sunday, and Lights Mobara December 13 alone. Preserve the calendar's event envelope and resolve competitive sessions from round detail/timetable pages. Do not silently subtract Friday or manufacture one date per numbered round.
- Parse `5月8日(金)-10日(日)` by inheriting month/year for the end. Accept Lights' literal `12月12日(土)-13(日)` with the omitted end-day suffix. Strip weekday annotations only after preserving the raw value; weekday mismatch is a review signal.
- Use 2026 from the verified heading/URL, never the publication date. Lights says updated December 15, **2025**; that is not its season. D1GP says updated January 15, 2026.
- Treat `予定` as provisional and T.B.A/TBN as unknown, not venue names or dates. `RD.1&2` is one shared weekend containing two rounds; retain both round identifiers without assigning their sessions automatically.
- Remove the registry's third-party `r.jina.ai` fallback from an official-only proposal. Direct official HTML was accessible during this inspection; no fallback was needed.

Assert heading prefix `Etapele Campionatului Național de DRIFT 2026` and suffix `Clasele PRO şi Semi-PRO` versus `CLASELE OPEN/STREET`.
