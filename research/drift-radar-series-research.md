## Implementation takeaway

Start with DMP, Drift Open, Drift Masters, Formula DRIFT, D1GP, and Baltic Drift because they expose the most usable recurring calendar data. Build a source adapter per series rather than a single schema assumption: the observed ecosystem is mostly HTML, with important exceptions being Google Sheets, PDF notices, and image/poster calendars. Store `source_url`, `retrieved_at`, `source_revision`/page date when available, `date_scope` (training/competition/full weekend), and a `needs_review` flag for conflicts or T.B.A. values.

No external accounts were accessed or modified.
